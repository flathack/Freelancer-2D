# Freelancer 2D

Freelancer 2D is a browser-based space sandbox inspired by the atmosphere and open-ended adventure of *Freelancer*. Explore star systems, trade commodities, dock at stations, build your reputation, upgrade your ship, and fight alongside or against traders, police, pirates, and other factions in a living 2D universe.

[Play Freelancer 2D](https://flathack.github.io/Freelancer-2D/)
